## 🇬🇧 English

A local full-pipeline production skill for short dramas and narrative video, treating the goal as a producible short-drama project rather than a standalone story. It supports two modes — cinematic live action and top-tier Chinese 3D animation CG — with mandatory style-and-tone locks, and moves through planning, script, assets, storyboard, keyframes, image-to-video prompts, voice and post-production, and self-check rework, with every step serving downstream generation: characters that can be drawn, scenes that can be reused, shots that can be produced, prompts a video model can execute, and a final cut that reaches editing and compositing. It can start from an idea, genre, outline, character relationships, an existing script, character art or raw assets, and includes actor-grade Chinese shot-number prompt formatting, character voice-reference planning, full preproduction snapshots with diff-patch synchronization, and end-to-end character-look and scene-state variant computation and acceptance across script, assets, video prompts, post-production and QC.

### ✨ What it does

- Starts from an idea, outline, existing script, character art or assets → a producible short-drama project
- Two modes — cinematic live action and top-tier Chinese 3D animation CG — with mandatory style-and-tone locks
- Asset, storyboard, keyframe, video, voice and post-production task packages are expanded in full, never abbreviated as "same as above" or "inherit the previous version"
- Each video_task_id yields one complete prompt containing multiple shot lines; per-shot singleton prompts are forbidden
- Character voice-reference planning plus actor-grade Chinese shot-number formatting aligned directly to the video model
- Full preproduction snapshots with diff-patch sync; character-look and scene-state variants are computed and accepted end to end
